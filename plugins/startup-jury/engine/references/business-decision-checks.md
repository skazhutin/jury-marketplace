# Additional business-decision checks

Apply these checks within the existing domains, not as extra agents, a score,
or an additional rejection threshold. Preserve stage awareness, evidence tracing,
the three-part fatal-flaw standard, and all existing verdict semantics.

## STANDALONE BUSINESS TEST

Distinguish GOOD PRODUCT / FEATURE, CREDIBLE STANDALONE BUSINESS, and, when
relevant, CREDIBLE SCALABLE COMPANY. Test independent value capture, customer
ownership, distribution potential, monetization, workflow position and expansion
surface. Consider feature-not-company and platform-native bundling risk, whether
this remains one feature in a suite, whether customers would maintain a separate
vendor relationship, and whether monetization supports the required organization.
Do not mechanically reject narrow products: a high-ACV vertical, profitable niche,
strong bootstrapped business or initial wedge can be compelling. Match business
form to the opportunity. Platform ability to copy is not by itself a fatal flaw.
Conclude STRONG / PLAUSIBLE / WEAK / NOT YET ESTABLISHED, with the decisive reason.

## COST OF LEARNING

For material unresolved decision-critical hypotheses, assess what must be spent
BEFORE a discriminating test is possible: time, engineering, regulatory work,
hardware/manufacturing, data acquisition, supply buildout, enterprise integrations,
certification, hiring and minimum operations. Classify CHEAP TO TEST / MODERATE /
EXPENSIVE / VERY EXPENSIVE / CAPITAL-INTENSIVE. Do not invent monetary precision
or exact timelines. Separate eventual economics from the cost of reaching evidence.
An attractive market can still require unaffordable learning. High uncertainty
can deserve VALIDATE FIRST when the decisive uncertainty is cheap to resolve.
For each decisive validation experiment add COST OF LEARNING, including when
material EXPECTED TIME / EFFORT TO EVIDENCE, CAPITAL / RESOURCE INTENSITY,
IRREVERSIBILITY, and CHEAPEST CREDIBLE TEST. Preserve all original experiment
fields, predeclared PASS / FAIL / INCONCLUSIVE and WHAT PASS WOULD NOT PROVE.
Cheap proxy evidence must not be portrayed as resolving an expensive technical test.

## FINANCING VIABILITY (conditional)

Distinguish BUSINESS VIABILITY from FINANCING VIABILITY only when external capital
is structurally required to reach viability. Particularly consider deeptech,
hardware, biotech/health where relevant, regulated infrastructure, manufacturing,
inventory, supply-intensive marketplaces and large upfront infrastructure.
Can realistic resources reach the next major proof point before exhaustion?
Identify capital-before-validation, the milestone for next financing, whether
that milestone requires unavailable capital, and dependencies on grants,
investors, strategic partners or large customers. Assess bootstrap/revenue
financing where plausible. Flag a financing dead zone as an evidence-backed risk,
not a presumed fact. Fundraising is not a universal criterion; difficult VC access
alone is not grounds for rejection. Credible financing and technical milestones
can support PURSUE for capital-intensive businesses.

## RIGHT TO WIN

Use only supplied or verified evidence about team access to customers,
distribution, difficult-to-access or proprietary data, technical/domain expertise,
regulatory knowledge, suppliers, audience/community, partnerships, capital,
infrastructure, credibility or unique insight. Conclude MEANINGFUL ADVANTAGE /
SOME ADVANTAGE / NO EVIDENT ADVANTAGE / UNKNOWN. With no team information write
UNKNOWN — do not infer. NO EVIDENT ADVANTAGE is not automatically fatal; many
startups start without a durable advantage. A strategy that necessarily requires
privileged access the team lacks creates a specific decision-relevant dependency.
Never invent founder attributes. Preserve USER-REPORTED status of supplied claims.

## EXPANSION LOGIC

Use INTENDED BUSINESS AMBITION in the frozen packet. For VENTURE-SCALE ask: if the
beachhead works extremely well, what credible mechanism makes this company
materially larger? Examine adjacent users/buyers, additional workflows,
geographies, products, platform/infrastructure expansion, increasing ACV or
monetization surface and relevant network/data advantages. Require a mechanism,
not “the TAM is huge.” For BOOTSTRAPPED / NICHE do not penalize absent venture-scale
expansion. Unknown ambition does not imply venture scale. An unproven moat alone
never prevents PURSUE.

## Cross-Examiner application

Explicitly assess the standalone business test and these possible contradictions:
- good product × no standalone company;
- strong customer value × insufficient value capture;
- venture-scale ambition × narrow expansion ceiling;
- attractive eventual market × prohibitively expensive validation;
- capital-intensive execution × no plausible financing path;
- strategy depends on privileged access × team lacks that access;
- cheap price × organization required is too expensive;
- initial wedge × no mechanism for expansion.

Establish whether each relevant conflict is real, applicable, fatal or non-fatal,
and testable, linking claim IDs and resolving evidence. Do not automatically call
these fatal. Also consider synergies, realistic financing and cheap learning that
strengthen the case. The existing fatal-flaw evidentiary standard still applies.

## Judge application and concise final report

Consider standalone business quality, cost of learning, conditional financing
viability, evidence-based right to win and ambition-relative expansion. Affect the
verdict only through a causal dependency, never arithmetic or a harsher default.
Keep the existing framework and final sections. Add STANDALONE BUSINESS TEST with
one conclusion and decisive reason; RIGHT TO WIN with its conclusion (UNKNOWN
when no evidence); COST OF LEARNING when material; EXPANSION LOGIC for venture-scale
ambition. Omit clearly irrelevant sections, and keep additions concise. Put
conditional financing findings in ECONOMIC REALITY or PRODUCT / EXECUTION REALITY
rather than adding another mandatory final section. Do not change verdict, analysis
status, confidence or business-outcome enums. Confidence is not success probability.
