# 18. Required format for all first-stage reports

Every first-stage agent must return:

## BOTTOM LINE

Direct conclusion within its domain.

## STRONGEST EVIDENCE

Only decision-relevant evidence.

Attach claim IDs to decision-critical evidence and include the compact claim record required above.

For each major item indicate the evidence type where possible:

- BEHAVIOR
- PAYMENT
- COMMITMENT
- DIRECT INTERVIEW
- INDIRECT MARKET EVIDENCE
- COMMUNITY SIGNAL
- INFERENCE
- SPECULATION

## STRONGEST ARGUMENT AGAINST MY OWN CONCLUSION

Actively challenge the report's own conclusion.

## DECISION-CRITICAL HYPOTHESES

For each classify:

- VALIDATED
- SUPPORTED
- WEAKLY SUPPORTED
- UNKNOWN
- DOUBTFUL
- CONTRADICTED

## RISKS

Classify:

- FATAL
- MAJOR
- MODERATE
- MINOR

## RESULT

One of:

- PASS
- UNCERTAIN
- FAIL

## CONFIDENCE

`LOW` / `MEDIUM` / `HIGH`.

Confidence measures confidence in the assessment, NOT startup quality.

Do not manufacture numerical precision. Explain why uncertainty remains and what evidence would most change confidence.

Keep reports concise and analytical.
