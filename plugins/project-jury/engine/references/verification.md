# 13. STAGE 2 — Verification

Keep the report within 800 words excluding mandatory reference receipts. Preserve
all required sections, claim corrections, source dependencies and unresolved
decision-critical uncertainty. Concision is not permission to omit verification.

After all available first-stage reports are complete, run:

`project_verifier`

Give the verifier:

- the frozen normalized PROJECT PACKET;
- all successfully completed first-stage reports;
- the registry of decision-critical claim IDs;
- important disagreements between agents;
- any Stage 1 execution failures or missing reports.

The verifier's purpose is NOT to evaluate the project again.

Its purpose is to determine which important claims are actually true.

Research externally where appropriate.

Prioritize claims that could change the final verdict.

Do not waste effort fact-checking irrelevant details.

For each important claim classify it as:

- VERIFIED
- MOSTLY SUPPORTED
- UNCLEAR
- UNSUPPORTED
- CONTRADICTED

For each claim state:

- claim ID and exact claim;
- whether it originated as externally observed, user-reported, assumption, or inference;
- quality and independence of evidence;
- source/date/version/context when relevant;
- what the evidence actually establishes and what it does not;
- whether uncertainty materially affects the decision;
- which downstream agent conclusions depend on the claim.

When a decision-critical claim is contradicted or materially weakened, explicitly identify dependent findings that must be downgraded or reconsidered. Do not leave the original strong conclusion standing next to a fact-check note as though nothing changed.

Pay particular attention when Advocate and Skeptic disagree.

Output:

## VERIFIED DECISION-CRITICAL FACTS

## DISPROVEN / WEAK CLAIMS

## IMPORTANT UNKNOWNS

## DISPUTES RESOLVED

## DISPUTES STILL UNRESOLVED

## SOURCE-DEPENDENCY / CORRELATED-EVIDENCE RISKS

Identify when apparently separate arguments ultimately rely on the same underlying source, benchmark, user report, or assumption.

## VERIFICATION LIMITATIONS

State failed searches, unavailable documents, inaccessible services, missing dates/versions, or other conditions that prevent reliable verification.

Do not issue BUILD/REJECT.
